# Route guide: Morabadi to Kanke Dam

A popular morning ride from Morabadi Ground north to the Kanke Dam reservoir, with views over the water at the end.

## 1 Overview

Length: about 4.5 km each way, 9 km return. Difficulty: moderate, with a steady climb for the last 1.5 km. Time: 25 to 35 minutes each way. Best bike: Trail Hybrid or Volt E-Bike; the City Cruiser manages but the climb is hard in one gear.

## 2 Stations

Start at the Morabadi Ground station. The Kanke Dam station sits at the end of the route, so you can finish there only if you started there: bikes go back to the station they left from.

## 3 The ride

### 3.1 Morabadi to Kanke Road

Leave Morabadi Ground on the north side and join Kanke Road. The first 3 km are flat with light traffic before 08:00.

### 3.2 The climb

After the turn towards the dam, the road climbs gently for about 1.5 km. Use a low gear and keep a steady pace. On a Volt, assist level 3 is enough.

### 3.3 At the dam

Lock the bike at the stands near the park gate. Riding on the dam wall itself is not allowed.

## 4 Tips

Go early: the reservoir is busy after 16:00 on weekends. There are tea stalls near the park gate but no water taps on the route; carry water.
