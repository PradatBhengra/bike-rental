# E-bike error codes

The Volt display shows a code when something is wrong. Most codes clear when you switch the bike off for 10 seconds and on again. If a code comes back, stop riding with assist, switch to level 0 and call the helpline on 0651-000-0000.

## 1 Battery codes

### 1.1 E01 Low battery

The battery is nearly empty. The motor stops helping, but you can keep pedalling. Ride back to your station at level 0.

### 1.2 E02 Battery too hot

The battery is above its safe temperature, usually after a long climb in the midday sun. Stop in the shade for 15 minutes with the bike switched off.

### 1.3 E03 Battery not connected

The battery is not seated properly. Do not try to open the battery cover; call the helpline and staff will guide you.

## 2 Motor and sensor codes

### 2.1 E10 Motor fault

The motor has stopped. Switch off and on once. If E10 returns, ride on at level 0 and report it at check-in.

### 2.2 E11 Speed sensor

The speed sensor on the rear wheel is not reading. Check that the small magnet on the spokes has not moved. The bike can still be pedalled.

### 2.3 E12 Brake sensor

A brake lever is reporting as pulled. Squeeze and release both levers a few times. If the code stays, the motor stays off for safety; ride on at level 0.

## 3 Display codes

### 3.1 E20 Communication error

The display has lost contact with the motor. Switch off, wait 10 seconds, and switch on again.

### 3.2 E21 Throttle or button stuck

A display button is stuck. Press each button once. The Volt has no throttle; the motor only helps while you pedal.
