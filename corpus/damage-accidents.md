# Damage, accidents and theft

## 1 Normal wear

Scuffs, worn brake pads, dirty chains and tyre wear from normal riding are our cost, not yours. Punctures are also covered unless the tyre was ridden flat for a long distance.

## 2 Damage

### 2.1 What counts as damage

Damage is harm beyond normal wear that happened during your rental: a bent wheel, a broken derailleur, a cracked e-bike display, a lost lock or key, or a missing light or bell.

### 2.2 How damage is charged

Staff note the damage at check-in and the repair cost is added as a due, capped at ₹3,000 for pedal bikes and ₹8,000 for e-bikes. You receive the itemised cost by email and can question it at the station within 7 days. We do not take a security deposit.

## 3 Accidents

### 3.1 What to do

Make sure everyone is safe first. If anyone is hurt, call 112. Then call the Saal Bikes helpline on 0651-000-0000 (06:00 to 22:00) and tell staff where you are.

### 3.2 Riding on after an accident

Do not ride a bike with bent wheels, loose handlebars or brakes that do not work. Lock it where it is safe and wait for staff, or push it to the nearest station.

## 4 Theft

### 4.1 If the bike is stolen

Report it to the nearest police station and get a copy of the FIR, then call the helpline. If the bike was locked with the supplied lock, you pay nothing. If it was left unlocked, you pay a theft charge of ₹5,000 for a pedal bike or ₹15,000 for an e-bike, as a due.

### 4.2 Lost keys and locks

A lost lock or key costs ₹500.
