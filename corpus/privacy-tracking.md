# Privacy and ride tracking

## 1 What we keep

We keep your name, email, phone number, your bookings and payments, and the messages you send to the chat assistant. Payment card details stay with Razorpay; we never see your card number.

## 2 Ride tracking

### 2.1 When tracking is on

Ride tracking is off unless you turn on tracking consent in your account. When it is on, the app records the phone's location only during an active ride, from pickup until return, while the ride page is open.

### 2.2 What tracking is used for

Tracking shows your route and distance on the ride page and in your ride history. Staff can see where bikes are during rides to help with breakdowns and overdue returns. We do not sell location data or use it for advertising.

### 2.3 Deleting your routes

You can delete your stored ride routes from your account page at any time. Raw location points are deleted after 90 days; the route summary stays until you delete it.

## 3 The chat assistant

The assistant can read your own bookings, rides and payments to answer your questions. It cannot see other customers' data. Messages are kept so we can improve answers; a thumbs down sends the answer to our review queue.
