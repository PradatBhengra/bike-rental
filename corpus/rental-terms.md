# Rental terms

These terms apply to every rental from Saal Bikes, a bike rental company in Ranchi. By booking you agree to them.

## 1 Who can rent

### 1.1 Age and account

You must be 18 or older and have a Saal Bikes account in your own name. The person who books is the person who rides. A friend may ride with you only on their own booking.

### 1.2 Identity check at pickup

At your first pickup, staff check a photo ID that matches the name on your account: Aadhaar, PAN card, passport, voter ID or a driving licence. We note the type of ID; we do not keep a copy.

### 1.3 Electric bikes

Volt e-bikes are limited to 25 km/h by the motor and need no driving licence. Riders of e-bikes must be 18 or older, the same as for every other bike.

## 2 Booking

### 2.1 How a booking works

You choose a station, a bike type and a start and end time. We hold one bike of that type for you while you pay. You book a type, not a particular bike; staff hand you any bike of that type at the station.

### 2.2 Booking limits

The shortest booking is 60 minutes and the longest is 72 hours. You can book up to 30 days ahead. You can hold at most 2 unpaid bookings at a time.

### 2.3 Opening hours

A booking must start and end while the station is open. Each station's hours are in the station guide and on the station page.

### 2.4 Paying for a booking

A new booking holds the bike for 15 minutes. Pay within the hold and the booking is confirmed; if you do not, the hold ends and the bike is released for other customers. All payments go through Razorpay.

## 3 Pickup and return

### 3.1 Collecting the bike

Show your booking code at the station desk. You can collect the bike up to 15 minutes before your booking starts. Check the brakes, tyres and lights with staff before you leave.

### 3.2 Returning the bike

Return the bike to the same station you collected it from. One-way rentals are not offered. Staff check the bike in and note its condition; your receipt is emailed a few minutes later.

### 3.3 Extending a ride

You can extend a confirmed or active booking from the app or the chat, as long as nobody has booked that bike straight after you. The extra time is priced on the same plan and added as a due, which you pay after the ride.

## 4 Prices and fees

### 4.1 How prices are worked out

Each started hour costs the hourly rate of the bike type. Every 24 hours of a booking is capped at the daily cap, so long rentals never cost more than the cap per day. Weekend and evening rates add a percentage to the hours they cover; the bike type pages show the current rates.

### 4.2 Dues

Late fees, extensions and damage charges become dues. A due is never taken from your card automatically: you pay it yourself on the Dues page. While you have an unpaid due you cannot make a new booking.

## 5 Your responsibilities

### 5.1 During the ride

Ride carefully, follow traffic rules and the riding guide, and lock the bike with the supplied lock whenever you leave it. You are responsible for the bike from pickup until staff check it back in.

### 5.2 Things you must not do

Do not lend the bike to anyone, carry a passenger, ride after drinking, take the bike outside Ranchi district, or use it for deliveries or racing.
