# Trail Hybrid manual

The Trail Hybrid is a light, fast bike with 21 gears and flat handlebars. It handles city roads and the paved lanes around Ranchi Lake and Kanke Dam.

## 1 Before you ride

### 1.1 Seat height

Open the quick-release lever under the seat, set the height so your leg is almost straight at the bottom of the pedal stroke, and close the lever firmly. Do not raise the seat post above its MAX mark.

### 1.2 Quick check

Check both brakes, press the tyres to feel that they are firm, and spin each wheel to see that it runs straight.

## 2 Gears

### 2.1 Using the 21 gears

The left shifter changes the front ring (1 to 3); the right shifter changes the rear gears (1 to 7). Use front 1 for steep hills, front 2 for most city riding and front 3 for fast flat roads. Shift only while pedalling, and ease off the pressure as you shift.

### 2.2 Chain noise

Avoid using the smallest front ring with the smallest rear gear, or the largest with the largest: the chain rubs and wears quickly. If the chain comes off, see the repair guide.

## 3 Brakes

The Trail Hybrid has mechanical disc brakes. The left lever is the front brake and the right lever is the rear brake. Disc brakes are strong: squeeze gently at first, and never use only the front brake on a downhill.

## 4 Specifications

Frame: aluminium. Gears: 3 x 7, 21 speed. Brakes: mechanical disc. Tyres: 700 x 38c. Weight: 13 kg. Rider height: 160 to 190 cm. Rack: rear, 10 kg.
