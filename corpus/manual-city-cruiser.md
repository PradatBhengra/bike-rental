# City Cruiser manual

The City Cruiser is a step-through town bike with an upright riding position, a front basket and a single gear. It suits short trips around Main Road, Lalpur and Morabadi.

## 1 Before you ride

### 1.1 Seat height

Loosen the quick-release lever under the seat, set the seat so your leg is almost straight with your heel on the pedal at its lowest point, then close the lever firmly. The seat post is marked MIN and MAX; never raise it above the MAX line.

### 1.2 Quick check

Squeeze both brake levers: they should stop before touching the handlebar. Press each tyre with your thumb: it should feel firm. Ring the bell and switch on the lights.

## 2 Riding

### 2.1 Gears

The City Cruiser has one gear, so there is nothing to shift. On steep climbs such as the road up to Kanke Dam, stand on the pedals or walk the bike.

### 2.2 Brakes

The left lever works the front brake and the right lever works the rear brake. Use both together and brake before corners, not in them. Brakes are weaker in rain; leave more room to stop.

### 2.3 Basket and load

The basket carries up to 5 kg. Do not hang bags from the handlebar; it makes steering unsafe.

## 3 Lights and lock

### 3.1 Lights

The front and rear lights are battery powered and switch on with the button on top of each light. Use them from sunset and in rain.

### 3.2 Lock

The cable lock is in the basket. Lock the frame to a fixed stand, not just the wheel. The key stays with the bike's key ring; losing it costs ₹500.

## 4 Specifications

Frame: steel, step-through. Gears: single speed. Brakes: front and rear rim brakes. Tyres: 26 x 1.75 inch. Weight: 17 kg. Rider height: 150 to 185 cm. Basket: front, 5 kg.
