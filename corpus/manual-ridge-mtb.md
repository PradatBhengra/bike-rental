# Ridge MTB manual

The Ridge MTB is a mountain bike with front suspension, wide knobbly tyres and 24 gears, built for rough tracks such as the trails near Kanke Dam and Tagore Hill.

## 1 Before you ride

### 1.1 Seat height

For climbing, set the seat so your leg is almost straight at the bottom of the pedal stroke. On steep descents, lower the seat a few centimetres for control. Close the quick-release lever firmly after every change.

### 1.2 Suspension

The front fork has a lockout lever on the right leg. Lock it on smooth roads to save energy and unlock it on rough tracks.

## 2 Gears

The left shifter changes the 3 front rings and the right shifter the 8 rear gears. Shift to an easy gear before a climb starts, not halfway up.

## 3 Brakes and tyres

### 3.1 Hydraulic disc brakes

The left lever is the front brake and the right lever is the rear brake. One finger on each lever is enough. On loose gravel brake gently with both, and keep your weight back on descents.

### 3.2 Tyre pressure

The tyres run at a lower pressure than road bikes for grip. If a tyre feels soft, ask any station to pump it; do not ride it flat.

## 4 Specifications

Frame: aluminium. Suspension: front fork, 100 mm. Gears: 3 x 8, 24 speed. Brakes: hydraulic disc. Tyres: 27.5 x 2.2 inch. Weight: 15 kg. Rider height: 160 to 190 cm.
