# Cancellations and refunds

## 1 Cancelling a booking

### 1.1 How to cancel

Open My bookings and choose Cancel, or ask the chat assistant. Before you confirm, the app shows exactly how much you will get back. You can cancel a booking that is waiting for payment or confirmed; once the bike is collected, the booking can no longer be cancelled.

### 1.2 Unpaid bookings

Cancelling a booking you have not paid for costs nothing. If you simply do not pay, the hold ends after 15 minutes and the booking expires on its own.

## 2 Refunds

### 2.1 Refund by notice given

The refund depends on how long before the start you cancel:

- Cancel 24 hours or more before the start: 100% refund.
- Cancel between 2 and 24 hours before the start: 50% refund.
- Cancel less than 2 hours before the start: no refund.

The notice is counted from the moment you press Cancel to the booked start time.

### 2.2 When the money arrives

Refunds go back to the card, UPI account or wallet you paid with. We send the refund to Razorpay straight away; banks usually show it within 5 to 7 working days. We email you when the refund is processed.

### 2.3 Payment that arrives after the hold

If a payment reaches us after your hold has ended, the booking stays expired and we refund the full amount automatically. You do not need to contact us.

## 3 Missed bookings

### 3.1 No-shows

If you have not collected the bike 60 minutes after the start, the booking is closed as a no-show and the bike is released. A no-show gets no refund.

### 3.2 When we cancel

If a bike cannot be handed over because of a fault or because the previous rider has not returned it, staff offer another bike of the same type. If there is none, we cancel the booking with a 100% refund.
