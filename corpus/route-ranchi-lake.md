# Route guide: Ranchi Lake loop

An easy, flat loop around Bada Talab (Ranchi Lake) in the heart of the old city. Good for a first ride or an evening out.

## 1 Overview

Length: about 2 km per loop. Difficulty: easy, completely flat. Time: 10 to 15 minutes per loop. Best bike: City Cruiser; any bike will do.

## 2 Stations

Start and end at the Ranchi Lake station on the west bank. Firayalal Chowk station is 1 km away if Ranchi Lake has no free bikes, but the bike must go back to the station it came from.

## 3 The ride

Ride the lakeside road anticlockwise. The east side passes the Swami Vivekananda statue; the south side is narrow, so slow down and ring the bell for walkers.

## 4 Tips

Evenings after 18:00 are lively and the road is lit. Weekend evenings are crowded: walk the bike on the busiest stretch near the statue.
