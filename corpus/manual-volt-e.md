# Volt E-Bike manual

The Volt E-Bike is a pedal-assist electric bike. The motor helps only while you pedal and stops helping at 25 km/h. It makes the climbs to Kanke Dam and Tagore Hill easy.

## 1 Before you ride

### 1.1 Switching on

Hold the power button on the left handlebar display for 2 seconds. The display shows the battery level, the assist level and your speed. If the display shows an error code, see the e-bike error codes guide before riding.

### 1.2 Seat height and checks

Set the seat as on any bike, check both brakes, and check that the battery shows at least two bars for your trip.

## 2 Pedal assist

### 2.1 Assist levels

Press + and - on the display to choose an assist level from 0 to 5. Level 0 turns the motor off. Levels 1 and 2 suit flat roads and save battery; levels 4 and 5 are for steep climbs. Start from a stop in level 1 or 2; level 5 can jerk the bike forward.

### 2.2 Walk mode

Hold the - button for 3 seconds to start walk mode, which moves the bike at walking speed while you push it up steps or a steep slope.

## 3 Battery and range

### 3.1 Range

A full battery gives about 60 km at assist level 1 to 2 and about 30 km at level 5. Heat, hills, a heavy load and low tyre pressure all shorten the range.

### 3.2 Charging

Riders do not charge the battery: every Volt leaves the station fully charged and staff charge it after the ride. If the battery runs out, the bike still pedals like a normal heavy bike. Do not open the battery cover.

## 4 Brakes

The Volt has hydraulic disc brakes; the left lever is the front brake and the right lever the rear. Pulling either lever cuts the motor at once.

## 5 Specifications

Motor: 250 W rear hub, assist up to 25 km/h. Battery: 36 V, 13 Ah. Range: about 30 to 60 km. Gears: 7 speed. Brakes: hydraulic disc. Weight: 24 kg. Rider height: 155 to 190 cm.
