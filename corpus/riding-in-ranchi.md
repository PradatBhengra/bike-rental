# Riding in Ranchi

These are Saal Bikes' safety rules for riding our bikes. They are company rules for rentals, not a summary of the law.

## 1 Safety rules

### 1.1 Helmets

Every station lends helmets for free; ask at the desk. We strongly recommend a helmet on every ride and require one on the Volt E-Bike and the Ridge MTB.

### 1.2 Lights and visibility

Use both lights from sunset to sunrise and in rain. Wear something light-coloured at night.

### 1.3 Phones and music

Do not hold a phone while riding. Stop at the side of the road to read directions. Keep at least one ear free of headphones.

## 2 Traffic

### 2.1 Busy roads

Main Road, Kutchery Road and the area around Firayalal Chowk are busy from 09:00 to 11:00 and 17:00 to 20:00. Keep to the left, watch for auto-rickshaws stopping suddenly, and walk the bike across large junctions if you are unsure.

### 2.2 Riding in a group

Ride in single file on busy roads. Leave at least one bike length between bikes.

## 3 Weather

### 3.1 Monsoon

From June to September roads can flood quickly. Do not ride through water deeper than the bottom of the wheel rim; you cannot see potholes under it. Brakes take longer to bite when wet.

### 3.2 Summer heat

April to June afternoons often pass 40 °C. Ride early in the morning or after 17:00, carry water, and rest in the shade. E-bike batteries also overheat in strong sun; see error code E02.

## 4 Where not to ride

Do not ride on footpaths, inside Birsa Zoological Park, on the Ranchi Ring Road highway, or on the dam walls themselves at Kanke and Hatia. Push the bike through crowded markets such as Upper Bazar.
