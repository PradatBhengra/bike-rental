# Station guide

Every station has a staffed desk, a pump, free helmets and a lock-up. Bikes return to the station they left from. Live free-bike counts are on the Find a bike page.

## 1 Morabadi Ground

Address: Morabadi Ground, east gate, Ranchi. Hours: 06:00 to 21:00. Landmark: opposite the stadium's east gate. Parking: two-wheeler parking beside the desk. The best start for the Kanke Dam route.

## 2 Ranchi Lake

Address: Bada Talab west bank, Ranchi. Hours: 06:00 to 21:00. Landmark: next to the boating ticket counter. Parking: street parking only. The start of the Ranchi Lake loop.

## 3 Kanke Dam

Address: Kanke Dam park gate, Kanke Road, Ranchi. Hours: 06:00 to 19:00. Landmark: by the park entrance. Parking: large car park at the gate. Closes earlier than the others because the park closes at dusk.

## 4 Firayalal Chowk

Address: Main Road, near Firayalal Chowk (Albert Ekka Chowk), Ranchi. Hours: 07:00 to 21:00. Landmark: beside the Albert Ekka statue. Parking: none; come on foot or by auto.

## 5 Ranchi Junction

Address: Ranchi railway station, main entrance, Station Road. Hours: 05:00 to 23:00. Landmark: left of the main entrance. Parking: station car park (paid). The longest hours of any station, for early and late trains.

## 6 Lalpur

Address: Lalpur Chowk, Circular Road, Ranchi. Hours: 06:00 to 21:00. Landmark: opposite the bus stop. Parking: limited street parking.

## 7 Harmu

Address: Harmu Housing Colony market, Ranchi. Hours: 06:00 to 21:00. Landmark: next to the Harmu post office. Parking: market parking.

## 8 Doranda

Address: Doranda, near the Hinoo turning, Ranchi. Hours: 06:00 to 21:00. Landmark: opposite the Doranda college gate. Parking: street parking.
