# Late returns and fees

## 1 Grace period

Every booking has a grace period of 15 minutes after its end time. A bike returned within the grace period costs nothing extra.

## 2 Late fees

### 2.1 How the late fee is worked out

If you return the bike after the grace period, you pay the late rate of your bike type for each started hour, counted from the booked end time. The late rate per hour is shown on each bike type page:

- City Cruiser: ₹60 an hour.
- Trail Hybrid: ₹80 an hour.
- Ridge MTB: ₹100 an hour.
- Volt E-Bike: ₹150 an hour.

For example, a City Cruiser returned 80 minutes late costs two started hours, ₹120.

### 2.2 Paying a late fee

The late fee is added to your account as a due when staff check the bike in. Pay it on the Dues page. New bookings are blocked until every due is paid.

## 3 Overdue rides

### 3.1 Reminders

When a ride passes its end time and the grace period, we email you once to remind you to return the bike. Staff can see overdue rides on their screens.

### 3.2 The next customer

If someone has booked your bike after you, we try to give them another bike of the same type. Returning late can leave another rider waiting, so please extend your booking in the app instead if you need more time.

## 4 Extending instead

Extending before your end time is cheaper than a late return: the extra hours cost the normal hourly rate instead of the late rate. You can extend only if nobody has booked the bike straight after you.
